BEFORE YOU START

You are powerful. You are not all-powerful.

The game will keep showing you the difference between what government controls, what it can only influence, and what other institutions decide.

✅You can

Set government priorities, propose laws, change many taxes, allocate spending and appoint ministers.

↔️You can influence

The economy, NHS performance, housebuilding, wages, investment and public behaviour.

⛔You cannot simply order

Interest-rate cuts, court verdicts, council planning approvals, House of Lords votes or foreign governments.
